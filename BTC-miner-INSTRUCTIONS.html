Plug & Play Quick Start Setup Guide

 SOLO MINER

Congratulations on receiving your standalone Micro BTC Miner! This tiny gadget connects directly to the Bitcoin network to mine blocks independently. Follow this simple 3-step guide to get up and running in under 2 minutes.

STEP 01
Power On & Connect to Setup Wi-Fi
Plug your device into any standard USB power source (5V power adapter or PC USB port).
  • When powered on for the first time, the screen will display SETUP MODE.
  • Take your smartphone, tablet, or laptop and open your Wi-Fi Settings.
  • Look for the network named:
    BTC-Miner-Setup
  • Connect to it. (No password is required).

STEP 02
Configure Connection & Bitcoin Wallet
Once connected to BTC-Miner-Setup, a setup page should automatically pop up on your smartphone or browser.
Portal didn't open automatically?
If your browser doesn't open automatically, open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.) and go to:
http://192.168.4.1

Fill in the configuration fields:

  1. WiFi Network (SSID): Select your home Wi-Fi network from the drop-down menu.
  2. WiFi Password: Enter your home Wi-Fi password.
  3. BTC Wallet Address: Paste your Bitcoin receiving wallet address here. (Crucial: If you hit a block, rewards go here!)
  4. Pool Host / Port / Worker: Pre-configured by default. You can leave them as is or customize your Worker Name.

Save & Start Mining!
Tap the "Save & Start Mining" button at the bottom of the portal page.

Troubleshooting & Reset Options
Automatic Setup Mode (Changing Wi-Fi)
If you change your Wi-Fi network or bring the device to a new location, simply turn off or disconnect your router. If the miner cannot connect to Wi-Fi within 15 seconds, it automatically re-enters SETUP MODE and broadcasts BTC-Miner-Setup again.
Manual Factory Reset (Hard Reset)
If you entered incorrect Wi-Fi credentials, typed a wrong wallet address, or cannot re-access the setup portal:
  1. Carefully remove the gadget from its plastic enclosure.
  2. On the board, you will see two micro-buttons labeled RST and BOOT.
  3. While the device is plugged in and powered on, press and hold the BOOT button continuously for 5 seconds.
  4. Release the button. The software memory will reset completely, and the device will restart in SETUP MODE (showing the BTC-Miner-Setup Wi-Fi network again).
